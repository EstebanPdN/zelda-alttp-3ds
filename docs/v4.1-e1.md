# Version 4.1-E1

Experimental pre-release. See the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

Fixes come from reports received after v4.0 and from a review of every language:
all 396 messages, item names, file screens, maps, Game Over, intro, epilogue and
credits. Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Russian text was corrected on top of the
original translations, whose authors keep their [credits](../CREDITS.md).
French and German remain the official cartridge text.

No physical Old or New 3DS has tested this build.
